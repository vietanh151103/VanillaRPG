VanillaRPG-Core 0.6.7 update

1. Stop the server before replacing the plugin JAR.
2. Replace the old VanillaRPG-Core JAR with VanillaRPG-Core-0.6.7.jar.
3. Keep the existing VanillaRPG-Core/config.yml and rpg.db. No manual database migration is needed.
4. Start the server, then use /shop or /shop history.

New features
- /shop now has Food, Building, and Sell minerals categories.
- The 36-slot sell chest shows the expected combined payout before selling.
- /shop history lists the 10 newest server-shop purchases and sales.
- Existing shop item entries without category keep working: food is inferred as FOOD,
  and the configured common blocks are inferred as BUILDING.
