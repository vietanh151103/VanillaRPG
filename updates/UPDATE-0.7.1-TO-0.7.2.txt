VanillaRPG-Core 0.7.2 update

1. Stop the server normally.
2. Replace VanillaRPG-Core-0.7.1.jar with VanillaRPG-Core-0.7.2.jar in server-template/plugins.
3. Keep plugins/VanillaRPG-Core/ and its rpg.db files; no player-data migration is required.
4. Start the server normally.

Every loaded hostile mob and dungeon mob now shows current/max HP above its name. Passive animals are excluded. Damage dealt by a player appears as a temporary floating number above the target. Configure it in config.yml:

mob-health-display:
  enabled: true
  update-interval-ticks: 10

The floating damage lasts 20 ticks by default:

mob-health-display:
  floating-damage:
    enabled: true
    duration-ticks: 20
