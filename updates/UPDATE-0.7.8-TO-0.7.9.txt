VanillaRPG-Core 0.7.9 - Open Player Market

- Re-enables the Player Market with Elite Coin transactions.
- Every serializable non-soulbound ItemStack can be listed, including EliteMobs and custom items.
- Soulbound EliteMobs items are rejected by both the command and GUI listing paths.

After installing

1. Replace the Core JAR with VanillaRPG-Core-0.7.9.jar.
2. Run /rpg reload after confirming market.enabled: true in config.yml.
