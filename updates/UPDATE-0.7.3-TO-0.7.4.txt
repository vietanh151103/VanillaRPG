VanillaRPG-Core 0.7.4 update

VanillaRPG rewards are now injected into newly generated vanilla and TerraformGenerator loot containers.

- Ancient City, End City, Bastion, stronghold, mineshaft and surface structure loot can receive RPG materials and rarity gear.
- The existing `betterstructures-loot` tier chances are reused for consistent economy balancing.
- `vanilla-loot.max-extra-items` limits supplemental reward stacks per container.
- Existing or already-opened chests are not reset and will not be filled retroactively.

Configuration:

```yaml
vanilla-loot:
  enabled: true
  max-extra-items: 2
```
